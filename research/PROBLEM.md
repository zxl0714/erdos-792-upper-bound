Let \(A\) be a finite set of integers. A subset \(B\subseteq A\) is sum-free if there are no \(x,y,z\in B\) satisfying \(x+y=z\), where the variables may coincide. For each positive integer \(n\), let
\[
f(n)=\min_{\substack{A\subseteq\mathbb Z\\|A|=n}}
        \max\{\,|B|:B\subseteq A\text{ is sum-free}\,\}.
\]
Determine the asymptotic order of \(f(n)-n/3\) up to absolute constant factors as \(n\to\infty\).
