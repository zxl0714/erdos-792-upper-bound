# Primary references

The [proof map](../proof/README.md) identifies where the mathematical inputs are used.

| Input | Role in the proof |
| --- | --- |
| [Grynkiewicz, popular sumsets](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11856-010-0053-6), [author's final text](https://www.diambri.org/Mathpdfs/pollard--vFIN.pdf) | Theorem 1.1 is used in the finite weighted exclusion and cyclic pair lemmas. |
| [Kneser's theorem, DeVos's account](https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.3539) | The stabilizer and saturation inequality for finite subsets of an abelian group. |
| [Griesmer, v3](https://arxiv.org/html/1807.01694v3) | Theorems 1.1 and 1.15 enter the single-pair cyclic lemma at fixed accuracy. |
| [Martin and Sitar](https://arxiv.org/html/0903.1834v1#S1.Thmtheorem2) | The constant-target specialization of Theorem 1.2 supplies a discrepancy estimate for consecutive blocks. |
| [Saxton and Thomason, v3](https://arxiv.org/html/1204.6595v3) | The container theorem is used in the rounding step for the Schur hypergraph. |

## Problem and prior work

- [Erdős Problem #792](https://www.erdosproblems.com/792) and [Green's open problems list](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/greenbj/papers/open-problems.pdf).
- [Eberhard, Green and Manners](https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.4579) establish the sharp leading term. The [published paper](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2014/180-2/p05) is a source for the weighted construction strategy.
- [Eberhard's multiplicative construction](https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.6390) gives an earlier effective approach.
- [Bedert](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08624) proves the lower excess $\Omega(\log\log n)$.
- The [upper-bound comparison](literature/run18-logarithmic-upper-literature-audit.md) and [lower-bound status audit](literature/run18-lower-status-audit.md) record the sources checked and the coverage of the literature search.
