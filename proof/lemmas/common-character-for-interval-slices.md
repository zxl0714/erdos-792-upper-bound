# A common character for interval approximations of slices

Let $G$ be finite cyclic, with uniform measure $m$, and let $A_s,B_t\subset G$ be measurable families for $s,t\in(0,1]$. Measurability means that membership of each group element is measurable in time. Put $\Delta=\{(s,t):s,t>0,\ s+t\le1\}$ and $d\mu=3(s+t)\,ds\,dt$.

**Lemma.** Fix $0<c<1/2$, $\epsilon>0$, and $N_0\ge1$. Assume that whenever two cyclic interval preimages have densities in $[c,1-c]$, defining characters of orders greater than $N_0$, and symmetric difference at most $2\epsilon$, their characters agree up to sign. The [uniqueness lemma](uniqueness-of-cyclic-characters.md) gives such a choice of $\epsilon,N_0$.

Let $0<\tau\le1/4$ and $0\le\delta\le\tau^3/100$. Suppose that outside a set of $\mu$-measure at most $\delta$, each $(s,t)\in\Delta$ has two interval preimages of densities in $[c,1-c]$ under the same character of order greater than $N_0$, approximating $A_s,B_t$ within $\epsilon$ in measure. Then there is one character $\chi_0$ of order greater than $N_0$ giving such an approximation for each family outside an exceptional time set of measure at most $\tau+64\delta/(3\tau^2)\le2\tau$. The approximants are measurable functions of time.

**Proof.** There are finitely many candidate triples of a character and two intervals. Choose the first whose approximation tests hold, on the measurable set $\Omega$ of admissible pairs. Its complement has $\mu$-measure at most $\delta$. Use Fubini-good row and column coordinates in all slice selections below, so the sections of $\Omega$ and the finite choices are measurable on the selected slices.

Set $I=[\tau/4,\tau/2]$ and $T=(0,1-\tau)$. The weight $3(s+t)$ is at least $3\tau/4$ on both $T\times I$ and $I\times T$, so each rectangle has at most $4\delta/(3\tau)$ bad planar measure. For column coordinates $v\in I$, the average bad length in $T$ is at most $16\delta/(3\tau^2)$. The same holds for rows $u\in I$. Put $H=64\delta/(3\tau^2)$. By Markov, at least $3\tau/16$ of each set of coordinates has bad length at most $H$. If $\delta=0$, the bad lengths vanish almost everywhere. The product of these two sets has area at least $9\tau^2/256$, while the bad measure in $I^2$ is at most $4\delta/(3\tau)\le\tau^2/75$. Choose a good intersection $(u_0,v_0)$ of an admissible row and column. Let $\chi_0$ be its chosen character.

For any $(s,v_0)\in\Omega$, its approximant to $B_{v_0}$ and that at $(u_0,v_0)$ are at distance at most $2\epsilon$. Thus their characters agree up to sign. Reflecting an interval in the negative-sign case realizes the corresponding approximant to $A_s$ under $\chi_0$ with the same error. Apply the row argument at $(u_0,t)$ by comparing approximants to $A_{u_0}$ to obtain the approximation for $B_t$. Each argument loses at most $H$ of $T$ plus the $\tau$ at the end of the time interval. The choices on these slices are measurable. $\square$
